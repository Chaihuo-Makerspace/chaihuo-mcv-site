# Yulin, Shaanxi

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Located where the Loess Plateau meets the Mu Us Sandy Land, Yulin is broadly divided by the Great Wall into northern sandy grasslands and southern loess hills and gullies, with terrain descending from west to east.
- Step: Located on China's second terrain step, at the transition between the northern Loess Plateau, the southern Ordos Plateau, and the Mu Us Sandy Land.
- Climate: Temperate arid to semi-arid continental monsoon climate with abundant sunshine, limited rainfall concentrated mainly in summer, and a marked day-night temperature range; representative urban elevation is about 1,085 metres.
- Challenge: Windblown sand, dry air, strong sunshine, and short-lived summer convective weather place sustained demands on vehicle dust protection, cooling, energy management, and crew hydration.
