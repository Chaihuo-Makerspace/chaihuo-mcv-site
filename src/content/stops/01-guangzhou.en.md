## Event

On 24 April the lab parked at the Guangdong Science Center. Rain came down hard; Mr. Chen from the Guangzhou University Town student-aid association arrived without an umbrella and sat in the van until it stopped. Teacher Wu took the crew to the science-center canteen. Saturday the 25th brought more families in the morning and a vibe-coding workshop in the afternoon. After saying goodbye to Teacher Wu, the lab left Guangzhou on the expressway for Yangjiang.
