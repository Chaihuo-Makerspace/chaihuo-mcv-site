# Hami

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Oasis of the Hami Basin on the southern slope of the eastern Tianshan, the gateway out of Xinjiang
- Step: Second Terrain Step
- Climate: Temperate continental arid climate, very hot in summer
- Challenge: The long exit from Xinjiang, plus a crew handover in 40 °C heat

## Activities

- Visit to the Hami Science and Technology Bureau
- Two-leg handover on the northern slope of the eastern Tianshan

## Event

On 8 June the crew took the high-speed train from Xining to Hami for a flash stop and visited the municipal science bureau. On 11 June they learned the lab was already in Golmud and that Urumqi freight would wait — so they went to pick it up. On 6 July Ye Yu finished the two-leg handover on the northern slope of the eastern Tianshan, unpacking gear in the shade by a stream, and drove the cleared van back to Hami at dusk. On 7 July they left Xinjiang on the Lianhuo Expressway on a full charge.
