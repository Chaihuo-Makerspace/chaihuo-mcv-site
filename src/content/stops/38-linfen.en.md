# Linfen

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Fen River valley (Linfen Basin), flanked by the Lüliang and Taiyue mountains
- Step: Second topographic step, faulted basin belt on the eastern Loess Plateau
- Climate: Warm temperate semi-humid continental monsoon climate, windy springs
- Challenge: Fast-changing road conditions across the basin-mountain transition; spring dust demands cooling and equipment protection
