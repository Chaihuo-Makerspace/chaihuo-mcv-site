# Taiyuan

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Taiyuan Basin with the Fen River running through, hemmed in by mountains east and west
- Step: Second topographic step, basin cluster in the central Loess Plateau
- Climate: Warm temperate continental monsoon climate, large diurnal range, dry autumn and winter
- Challenge: Basin temperature inversions and autumn-winter haze; heavy urban traffic complicates scheduling and resupply
