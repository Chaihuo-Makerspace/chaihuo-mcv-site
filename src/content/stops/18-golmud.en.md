# Golmud

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Southern edge of the Qaidam Basin, northern gateway to the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau
- Step: First Terrain Step
- Climate: Plateau continental arid climate
- Challenge: Vehicle resupply, power stability, and communications after a long high-altitude transfer

## Activities

- Pickup after the Qinghai-Tibet Railway
- Drive into Xinjiang from Golmud instead of waiting on freight

## Event

On 7 June the crew rode the Qinghai-Tibet Railway north and reached Golmud after 9 a.m., past Kunlun Pass. On 11 June, still in Hami, they learned the lab had already been shipped to Golmud and that a onward truck to Urumqi would take about ten days — so they went to pick it up themselves and drive into Xinjiang. On the morning of the 12th they charged in Golmud, bought large baked huajuan, saw the molten-salt solar tower on the skyline, and left town on the S318 into the Gobi toward Mangya.
