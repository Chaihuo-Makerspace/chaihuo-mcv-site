## Event

On 10 July the lab ran from Dunhuang to Yumen. At a service-area charge they pulled the side door and set up laptops — a mobile hackerspace on the Gobi. Organized by the Yumen Association for Science and Technology, the Chaihuo lab, the Yumen science van, and the Gansu Science Museum van rolled into Yumen No. 2 Middle School and opened a vibe-coding hardware workshop to students for the first time.
