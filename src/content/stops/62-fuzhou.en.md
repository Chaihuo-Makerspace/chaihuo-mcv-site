# Fuzhou

## Event

A 650 km, 10-hour transfer day from Wuxi: the van left at 10:30, crossed the layered mountains of southern Zhejiang via Taishun, chased the evening clouds south, and rolled through the Fuzhou North (Guihu) toll gate at 20:30. The first thing seen on entering the city was a street vendor still minding her stall, head down over her phone.
