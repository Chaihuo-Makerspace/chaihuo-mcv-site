# Mangya

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Western edge of the Qaidam Basin, with desert, salt lake, and yardang landscapes
- Step: First Terrain Step
- Climate: Plateau desert arid climate
- Challenge: Thermal management, sand protection, and energy scheduling across long desert drives

## Activities

- Side stop at Emerald Lake
- Dawn visit to Aiken Spring, the "Eye of the Devil"

## Event

On 12 June the lab ran about 460 km of Gobi on the S318 from Golmud to Mangya. A salt flat appeared beside the road; at 4:30 p.m. they pulled up at Emerald Lake. After 7 p.m. they entered town — nodding donkey pumps lining the road, Gaskule Lake on the horizon. Mangya became a city in 2018, often called China's loneliest. That night they decided to see Aiken Spring, the "Eye of the Devil," at dawn.
