# Qinhuangdao Transit

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Where the Yan Mountains meet the Bohai coastal plain, following the Liaoxi Corridor southwest
- Step: China's third topographic step, connecting the Northeast and North China plains
- Climate: Warm-temperate semi-humid continental monsoon climate with a strong coastal influence
- Challenge: A same-day long-distance transfer requiring stable vehicle, equipment, and crew operations

## Activities

- Recorded only as a route transit point between Shenyang and Tianjin, not as a formal city stop

## Event

On August 23, 2026, the MCV passed through Qinhuangdao in Hebei while travelling south from Shenyang to Tianjin.

## Expedition Log

### World

The route crossed from Liaoning into Hebei and continued southwest along the Bohai coast.

### Fire

The transit point keeps the driven route geographically complete.

### Frontier

Without stopping in Qinhuangdao, the MCV continued toward Tianjin.
