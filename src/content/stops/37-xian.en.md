# Xi'an

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Located in the central Guanzhong Plain, Xi'an is bordered by the Wei River to the north and the Qinling Mountains to the south; its urban area is dominated by alluvial plains and loess tablelands, generally descending from southeast to northwest.
- Step: Located on the eastern edge of China's second terrain step in the Guanzhong Basin, where the Qinling Mountains and the Wei River Plain form a clear geographic boundary.
- Climate: Warm-temperate, semi-humid continental monsoon climate with four distinct seasons, hot rainy summers, and cold dry winters; representative urban elevation is about 400 metres.
- Challenge: Midsummer heat, short intense rainfall, and the urban heat-island effect place sustained demands on vehicle cooling, energy management, equipment waterproofing, and crew hydration.
