# Litang

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Maoya grassland of the Shaluli Range, the "world's highest town"
- Step: Third Terrain Step
- Climate: Plateau cold-temperate
- Challenge: Onboard electronics stability and crew altitude-sickness protection under near-4,000 m hypoxia and low pressure

## Activities

- Lunch and charging in Litang, the "world's highest town"
- Transit via Kazila Pass and the Wuliang River wetland

## Event

On 27 May the lab left Yajiang, crossed the Eighteen Bends, Xiongzongka (4,280 m) and Kazila Pass (over 4,700 m), and dropped into Litang. At noon the crew charged and ate lunch above 4,000 m, passing through the East Gate of the "world's highest town." After a morning at altitude, walking back from the charger brought dizziness. After lunch they left by the West Gate toward the Wuliang River wetland, the Twin Lakes, and Batang.
