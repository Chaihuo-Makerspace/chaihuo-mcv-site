# Bomi

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Parlung Tsangpo valley, "land of glaciers" between the Nyenchen Tanglha and Himalayas
- Step: Third Terrain Step
- Climate: Plateau temperate humid (southeast Tibet's "green triangle")
- Challenge: Passage assessment and contingency on the geo-hazard-prone (mudflow, landslide) stretch of the Sichuan–Tibet route

## Activities

- Arrival after the Nujiang 72 bends
- Overnight in Zhamo, with the Songzong snow-forest stretch

## Event

On 29 May the lab left Zuogong, crossed Yela Pass (4,650 m) and the Nujiang 72 bends, aiming for Bomi. The road along Ranwu Lake was broken; it only improved near town. After 6 p.m. they reached Songzong, then Zhamo at 2,720 m — a full 1,000 m lower than Zuogong, the air suddenly humid. The Parlung Tsangpo runs through town, ringed by snow mountains, glaciers, and old forest.
