# Lankao

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Lower Yellow River alluvial plain, low and flat, with sand ridges and backswamps
- Step: China's third topographic step, on the eastern Henan plain
- Climate: Warm-temperate continental monsoon climate, still warm in early autumn
- Challenge: Leaving the Shandong coast for the Henan hinterland requires a long cross-province transfer

## Activities

- Arrived in Lankao, the first stop in Henan, to continue local connections and prepare for the next departure

## Event

On September 5, 2026, the mobile lab arrived in Lankao, Henan.
