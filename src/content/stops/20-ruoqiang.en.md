# Ruoqiang

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Southeastern edge of the Tarim Basin, transition zone below the Altyn-Tagh Mountains
- Step: Second Terrain Step
- Climate: Warm temperate continental extremely arid climate
- Challenge: Temperature swings, dust, and continuous transfer pressure after descending from the plateau

## Activities

- First stop after entering Xinjiang
- Loulan Museum visit

## Event

On 13 June, after a dawn stop at Aiken Spring, the lab took the G315 toward Ruoqiang. Around 10:30 a.m. a roadside sign read "Welcome to Xinjiang." They topped up in Yitunbulake, joined the Xihe Expressway, reached Ruoqiang at noon, and visited the Loulan Museum. At 6 p.m. they left the museum and continued toward Korla on the Wuo Expressway.
