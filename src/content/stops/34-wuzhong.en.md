# Wuzhong

## Telemetry

- Terrain: The Yellow River crosses the city, with an alluvial plain in the north, the Ordos platform to the east, and loess hills and gullies to the south, alongside mountains, foothills, and reservoir areas.
- Step: Located on China's second terrain step, at the transition between the Loess Plateau, the Ordos Plateau, and the Ningxia Plain.
- Climate: Mid-temperate arid to semi-arid continental climate with ample sunshine, limited rainfall, high evaporation, and a marked day-night temperature range; the irrigated plain averages about 1,200 metres above sea level.
- Challenge: Windblown sand, dry air, and strong sunshine demand careful dust protection, cooling, and crew hydration, while the dense irrigation network requires considered route planning.
