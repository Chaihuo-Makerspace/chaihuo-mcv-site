# Nyingchi

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Confluence valley of the Yarlung Tsangpo and Niyang rivers, "Tibet's Jiangnan"
- Step: Third Terrain Step
- Climate: Plateau temperate semi-humid monsoon
- Challenge: Equipment behavior in the humid Lulang–Nyingchi valley; crossing Sejila Pass

## Activities

- Stone-pot chicken stop in Lulang
- Crossing Sejila Pass with the Niyang River alongside

## Event

On 30 May the lab left Bomi at dawn along the Parlung Tsangpo valley. Around noon they reached Lulang — "Valley of the Dragon King" in Tibetan, nicknamed the Switzerland of the East — for stone-pot chicken. At 3 p.m. they crossed Sejila Pass (4,700 m), then took the Yayye Expressway past Nyingchi without stopping in the city, the Niyang River beside the road.
