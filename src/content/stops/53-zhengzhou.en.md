# Zhengzhou

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Middle and lower Yellow River alluvial plain, higher in the west and lower in the east, with the Mangshan hills to the northwest
- Step: China's third topographic step, on the central Henan plain
- Climate: Warm-temperate continental monsoon climate, still warm in early autumn
- Challenge: Moving from Kaifeng into Zhengzhou means shifting from an old-city scene to a provincial-capital campus makerspace
