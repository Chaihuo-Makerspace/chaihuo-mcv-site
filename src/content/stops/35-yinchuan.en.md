# Yinchuan

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Located in the central Ningxia Plain, between the Helan Mountains and the Ordos Plateau, the city occupies alluvial and piedmont plains shaped by the Yellow River and is interwoven with canals, lakes, and wetlands.
- Step: Located on China's second terrain step, where the Helan Mountain piedmont meets the upper Yellow River alluvial plain.
- Climate: Mid-temperate arid continental climate with limited rain and snow, abundant sunshine, high evaporation, and marked daily and seasonal temperature ranges; the urban area averages about 1,100 metres above sea level.
- Challenge: Dry air, windblown sand, and strong sunshine test vehicle dust protection and thermal management, while the plain's waterways and wetlands require attention to road conditions and ecological protection.
