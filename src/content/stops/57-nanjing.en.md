## Activities

## Event

After wrapping up the Hangzhou maker sessions the lab drove 200-odd kilometres north to Nanjing, settling in at the Bairi Fangzhou resort in Xianlin as a showcase guest and the open-hardware and technical-training provider for Insta360's four-day hackathon. Cleaning the van on the way in turned up faded, peeling decals, so replacements were rush-ordered and swapped on before the opening.
