# Yiwu

## Event

The van ran nearly 400 km of highway from Wuxi to Yiwu, arriving around 6 p.m. in the small-commodity capital. At a tight, truck-clogged intersection it clipped a parked lorry — the first real scrape of the trip. The truck was unhurt, the driver waved them on, and Feng said a van that has come this far would look unfinished without a mark.
