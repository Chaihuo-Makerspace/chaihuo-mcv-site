# Jinan

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Northern foothills of Mount Tai on the lower Yellow River alluvial plain, higher in the south and lower in the north, with springs scattered through the city
- Step: China's third topographic step, in the eastern North China Plain
- Climate: Warm-temperate semi-humid continental monsoon climate, still hot in late summer
- Challenge: The southbound transfer from Tianjin into Shandong requires stable onboard systems between expressway driving and provincial-capital streets

## Activities

- Arrived in Jinan, the first formal stop in Shandong, to continue maker connections, public exchange, and the next departure

## Event

On August 31, 2026, the mobile lab left Tianjin and arrived in Jinan, Shandong.
