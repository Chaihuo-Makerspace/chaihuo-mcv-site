# Tianjin

## Telemetry

- Terrain: Northeastern North China Plain at the lower Hai River, bordering the Bohai Sea with predominantly low, flat terrain
- Step: China's third topographic step, in the coastal part of the North China Plain
- Climate: Warm-temperate semi-humid continental monsoon climate with a clear Bohai influence
- Challenge: The long transfer from Shenyang via Qinhuangdao requires smooth transitions between expressway and urban driving
