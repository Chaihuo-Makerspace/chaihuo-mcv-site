# Weihai

## Telemetry

- Terrain: The easternmost tip of the Shandong Peninsula, facing the Yellow Sea on three sides, with low hills meeting a coastal plain
- Step: China's third topographic step, along the Jiaodong hills and coast
- Climate: Warm-temperate humid monsoon climate moderated by the Yellow Sea, milder at the turn of summer and autumn
- Challenge: Crossing the Jiaodong Peninsula from Jinan to Weihai is a long in-province transfer

## Activities
